My main expertise is in cryptology.
  • I am one of the early researchers in India to have started active research in cryptology within the country. At the national level, I have pioneered research in several important areas of cryptology.
  • For some important cryptographic functionalities, solutions developed by me and my collaborators are the state-of-the-art.
Apart from cryptology, I work on a variety of other topics including mathematical aspects of economics, Boolean functions and discrete mathematics.
